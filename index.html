Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about targets, hours, and how you get paid.

Do I have to meet a sales target?

No. There's no target-based pressure — your job is to make guided calls on assigned leads. Consistency and call quality matter more than hitting a number.

What are the working hours?

Part-time is 3–4 hours a day, full-time is 5–6 hours a day. Shifts are flexible and Sundays are off.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts run on a weekly cycle. Full-time telecallers can earn up to ₹7,500 a week, tracked through the company dashboard.

Is the ₹49 joining charge refundable, and are there other charges later?

The ₹49 is a one-time joining charge to activate your account. There are no hidden or recurring charges after that.

Do I need prior telecalling experience?

No. Freshers, students, and homemakers join regularly — practical training and fixed scripts get you ready before your first live call.
